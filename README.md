# _In Silico_ Evaluation of _De Novo_ Binders Targeting the Ricin B-Chain: Successes in Pocket-Targeting vs. Challenges in Shallow Epitopes
This repository documents the computational pipeline and structural evaluation of _de novo_ protein binders designed against the Ricin B-chain (PDB: 2AAI). The objective was to design miniprotein binders targeting three independent carbohydrate-binding subdomains—Y248, W37, and Y78—to block the toxin's cell entry mechanism. I successfully designed a high-affinity binder for the Y248 pocket (ipTM: 0.84). However, the shallow surface epitopes (W37 and Y78) proved highly resistant to _de novo_ design. Despite utilizing advanced generative strategies like Beta-sheet models, guiding potentials, and partial diffusion, the binders for W37 and Y78 capped at an ipTM of ~0.4. By comparing deep pockets and shallow surfaces, this repository presents a systematic investigation into how target geometry influences the success of AI based binder design, providing a methodological framework for future campaigns.
